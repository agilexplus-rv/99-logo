ninetynine wordmark source typeface: Inter Tight, weight 400 for "ninety" and 750 for the final "nine".
The symbol-and-wordmark lockups use the same outlined lettering.
The logo is an outlined rendering with optical tracking applied; no font is needed to display the SVG.
Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/intertight

The palette and website display fonts can change independently of the outlined logo geometry.
