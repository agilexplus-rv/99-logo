Quoting and order intake

Dear Elena,

Thank you for showing us how your team prepares customer quotations. We propose starting with the steps that require repeated copying between spreadsheets, emails and your accounts system.

Our first stage will document the current process, the information people need and what a useful improvement would look like. We will agree the scope, assumptions and definition of done before preparing a written proposal.

If the proposal is accepted, we can design and build the software, help your team adopt it and agree the hosting, maintenance and enhancement arrangements needed after launch.

We will review the quoting workflow with your operations team first. Order intake and reporting can then be considered as separately agreed next steps.
