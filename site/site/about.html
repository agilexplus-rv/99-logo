About

A small senior team,
and no wall to throw things over.

We are between five and twenty people by design. The person who scopes your work is the person who builds it, hosts it, and answers the phone when it misbehaves.

What that buys you

Not a bigger team. A shorter distance between the decision and the person affected by it.

Most delivery failures are not technical. They are translation failures: the person who understood the problem is not in the room when it is built, so the build answers a question nobody asked. Removing the handover removes the failure mode.
