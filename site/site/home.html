Why 99

99 is the last number
you need to count to.

You count to 99, then you stop counting and start using the thing. It is the point where a system stops being a project and starts being infrastructure — and the point where most firms leave.

We are not arriving there. We were there at one, at forty, at ninety. That is the whole argument: the same small senior team is accountable from the first conversation to the last number, and the next count after it.

We never claim 100. Not because we are incomplete, but because there is always one more count worth starting.

For companies that want to digitalise how they actually work — and stay ahead of the ones that won't — 99 is a small senior team that owns the whole count, under one accountable name.
