# 99 — Design Brief

## 1. The facts
- **Name:** 99 (numerals, spoken "ninety-nine")
- **What they do:** Software development company — but the promise is *delivered solutions*, not shipped code.
- **Audience:** Founders, CTOs and procurement leads buying an outcome under pressure. They have been burned by
  agencies that delivered a repository and called it done.
- **Decision-maker:** Company principals.
- **Where it must work:** GitHub org avatar (16–40 px), proposal covers, terminal/README, website header, business
  card, on a building.

## 2. Adjectives (the visual cues)
Precise · outcome-driven · understated · engineered · credible. **Not:** playful, futuristic, corporate-generic.

## 3. The strategic tension
"99" is the highest two-digit number — near-complete, one step from 100. That is the brand's whole argument:
*we take it to done.* The mark should carry **completion as an outcome**, not verbs about coding.

## 4. Category clichés — off limits
Angle brackets `< >`, `{ }`, gears/cogs, circuit traces, clouds, rockets, lightbulbs, brains+wires, hexagons
holding a letter, swooshes, binary streams, "digital" particles, infinity loops.

## 5. Constraints
- Must survive 16 px as a one-colour solid.
- No live `<text>` in final files; no gradients, filters, rasters.
- Maximum two colours + neutral.

## 6. Word map
| Input | Nouns / metaphors | Opposites |
|---|---|---|
| 99 | ninetieth-nine, near-complete, last mile, one short | first draft, WIP, backlog |
| Software | loop, cycle, build, compile, run, iterate | broken build, abandoned repo |
| Solutions | outcome, delivered, done, crossed the line, check | delivered code, handover doc, "as per spec" |
| Adjectives | plumb line, baseline, exact 45°, machined edge | sketchy, approximate, decorative |

**Intersection:** the moment the loop stops building and the work leaves — *the loop plus the lift*.
