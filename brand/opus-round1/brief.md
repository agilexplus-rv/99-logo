# 99 — Opus round 1: brief

**Name:** 99 (numerals). **Confirmed:** software development company; the promise is *delivered solutions, not just code*.
Everything else here is an assumption and is labelled as one.

**Assumed adjectives:** precise · confident · credible · understated. Not playful, not futuristic.

**What earlier rounds did:** a checkmark tail, stencil/counterform numerals, chamfered and custom-cut "99"
wordmarks. They are all ways of drawing the number nicely. This round looks for an *idea* the number already contains.

**Insight:** typographers teach quotation marks as "66 … 99". The **closing** quote mark is literally a 99.
It is the mark that ends a statement: the brief has been heard, and the thing is delivered.
Related ideas mined from the same word map: *turnkey* delivery, open loop → closed loop.

**Off limits:** brackets `< > { }`, gears, circuits, clouds, rockets, bulbs, hexagons, swooshes, infinity loops,
plain checkmarks and arrows, and the "99 % = almost done" reading (the promise is complete delivery).

**Name-adjacent marks to stay clear of:** [99x](https://99x.io/) (software engineering), [99designs](https://99designs.com/)
(design marketplace). This is not trademark clearance; a professional search is still required.
