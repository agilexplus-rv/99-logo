the direction is a real strategic choice rather than a shade.

- **A — Delivered Nine** (#1, letterform)
- **B — Completion Ring** (#2, abstract symbol)

Rejected: #4–#12 — too busy at 16 px (#4, #5, #6, #7), too literal (#8), gimmick (#9), or too quiet to own (#10, #12).
