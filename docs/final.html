The story

Most firms hand over the alphabet.
We stay for the rest of the language.

Every project has an A. A first conversation, a first line of code, a process that still runs on paper.

Most firms are excellent at the A. They are brilliant at the Z — the launch, the demo, the invoice. What they are not good at is everything after Z: the hosting that has to stay up, the dependency that needs upgrading in eighteen months, the enhancement the business needs the moment it changes shape.

99 exists for after Z.

We are a small senior team, so nothing is thrown over a wall — there is no wall. The people who scope your work are the people who build it, host it, and keep it alive. Software, hosting, maintenance, enhancements: one accountable name from the first letter to the last number.

And the name. 99 is the last number you need to count to. You count to 99, then you stop counting and start using the thing. It is the point where a project stops being a project.

We never claim 100 — not because we are incomplete, but because there is always one more improvement worth making, and that is precisely the work we are signing up for.

For companies that want to digitalise how they actually work — and stay ahead of the ones that won't — 99 is the small senior team that takes a process from A to Z and then keeps it running: software, hosting, maintenance and enhancements, under one accountable name.

Three final directions

Choose one.
Each is a different strategy, not a different drawing.

All three read as “ninety-nine” without a wordmark and survive one colour. They differ in what they ask the rest of the identity to do.

01

The Sign

Idea-led · typographic sign

The idea
A closing quotation mark is a 99. It is the sign that ends the statement.
Why it carries the story
The name is the symbol, so a lockup has no redundant “9 + 99”. Best small-size behaviour of the three: it still reads as punctuation at 16 px.
Honest risk
Quotation marks are common glyphs. Ownership comes from the drawn tail and consistent use.
Decision
Pick it if you want the name itself to be the symbol.

02

The Wordmark

Craft-led · custom wordmark

The idea
Two drawn numerals: round bowls, decisive swept tails, flat-cut terminals.
Why it carries the story
The plainest reading of the name, and the warmest. Nothing to decode, so it survives being printed small on an invoice footer.
Honest risk
Quiet. It asks the rest of the identity to carry the personality.
Decision
Pick it if you want quiet authority and zero misreading.

03

The Seal

Strategy-led · negative-space emblem

The idea
Two negative-space 9s carved through one solid, chamfered monolith.
Why it carries the story
Reads as finished and sealed — the A-to-Z promise as a single mass. Works as an app icon, avatar or stamp with no extra container.
Honest risk
Dense. The channels thin below 24 px, so it needs a small-size cut; reversed weight needs a nudge.
Decision
Pick it if you want a mark that doubles as an icon and a stamp.
