# 99 Invest Ltd · proposed company activities

**Draft for registration review — not a statement of registered objects or legal advice.** The wording below describes the intended software and technology business. Adapt it to the relevant jurisdiction, registry form and constitutional documents, and obtain appropriate legal review before filing. No NACE or other activity classification code is asserted.

## Short description for a form

The development, supply, licensing, deployment and operation of software, web applications, artificial-intelligence and machine-learning systems, and software-as-a-service products, including installation on customer premises; software maintenance, support, updates and enhancement; hosting, cloud infrastructure and managed technology operations; systems integration, data processing and reporting; business-process digitalisation, optimisation and automation; the supply and installation of related computing hardware and devices; and related technology consultancy, implementation and training services.

## Fuller proposed objects

The proposed activities of **99 Invest Ltd** are:

1. **Software discovery and development:** to analyse requirements, scope, research, design, architect, develop, configure, test, implement and integrate software, websites, web and mobile applications, application programming interfaces and associated digital systems for commercial and organisational use.
2. **Software supply and commercial operation:** to supply, distribute, license and operate proprietary or appropriately licensed third-party software and digital products, including subscription-based software-as-a-service and hosted applications, and to provide related implementation services.
3. **Maintenance and enhancement:** to provide ongoing software maintenance, technical support, fault investigation and correction, updates, upgrades, compatibility improvements and functional enhancements, including the modernisation of existing systems.
4. **Hosting and managed operations:** to provide, arrange, configure and manage hosting and cloud infrastructure, application deployment, backups, recovery arrangements, monitoring and associated operational support for software and digital systems, subject to agreed service terms.
5. **Business-process improvement:** to analyse and improve business workflows through digitalisation, systems integration, automation and the implementation of software-based tools, with the aim of improving operational efficiency and the use of information.
6. **Data and reporting:** to develop and implement data migration, processing, integration, analysis, reporting and dashboard solutions, subject to applicable data-protection requirements and client authorisations.
7. **Artificial intelligence:** to design, develop, train, fine-tune, evaluate and integrate artificial-intelligence and machine-learning models and systems, including local or on-premises deployment on customer-controlled infrastructure, inference operation, data preparation and model-governance activities, subject to the applicable AI, data-protection and product-safety rules.
8. **Consultancy and training:** to provide technology consultancy, technical assessments, solution planning, implementation guidance, documentation and user or administrator training relating to the foregoing activities.
9. **Ancillary activities:** to acquire or license the equipment, software and intellectual property required for these activities, engage relevant suppliers and contractors, and undertake lawful activities directly incidental to carrying them out.

## Scope safeguards

The word “Invest” in the company name does **not** establish or imply an investment business. These proposed objects do not describe investment management, securities dealing or holding, financial advice, payment services or other regulated financial activities. They do not claim authority to provide regulated legal or accountancy services, nor any security certification or accreditation.
